## What Yuuri can do

- **AI chat**: talk to Yuuri by saying her name or mentioning her. She replies in her own personality, in Thai or English.
- **Memory**: she remembers things you tell her (likes, hobbies, facts about you) so conversations feel personal. Memories are kept separately for each server.
- **Relationship**: Yuuri likes you more (or less!) depending on how you treat her.
- **Voice chat**: talk to Yuuri out loud in a voice channel.
- **Music**: play music in voice channels.
- **Web search**: she can look things up and read links you share.
- **Reaction Roles**: members get roles by reacting to a message with an emoji. Remove the reaction to remove the role.

## Getting started

1. [Invite Yuuri](https://discord.com/oauth2/authorize?client_id=1294900467888820325) to your server.
2. Run `/settings` to set up Yuuri for your server.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/help` | View the list of commands. |
| `/settings` | Set up Yuuri for your server. |
| `/set_embed` | Create a custom welcome embed. |

## Your privacy

Yuuri stores only what she needs to chat with you. You can turn off memory or ask for your data to be deleted at any time.
